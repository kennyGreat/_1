The process

How data installment works on DataBulge

Simple, transparent and fully in your control.

Create a free DataBulge account

Sign up with your phone number and email in under 2 minutes. A personal wallet and virtual bank account are created automatically on login.

Select your data plan

Browse MTN, Airtel and Glo data plans. Each listing shows the full plan cost and the minimum first installment needed to activate.

Fund your wallet and subscribe

Transfer the first installment to your DataBulge virtual account. Your plan activates immediately — complete the balance and your data is delivered automatically.

Complete your balance — data is delivered

Once your full balance is cleared, your data allocation is pushed to your SIM within seconds. No delays, no manual steps needed.

Clear the balance at your pace

Top up your wallet whenever you have funds. Your remaining balance reduces automatically and you are notified of every update.

Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

When do I receive my data?

DataBulge delivers your full data allocation the moment you complete your balance. Your plan activates with the first installment — data is pushed to your SIM automatically once the full amount is paid.

What networks support data installment?

DataBulge currently in Nigeria supports MTN Nigeria, Airtel Nigeria, Glo Nigeria and 9mobile Nigeria. More networks are actively being integrated.

How transparent is DataBulge about pricing?

DataBulge is transparent about pricing. The plan price shown is the total you pay. Open the app to see the full installment breakdown before subscribing.

How do I pay my installments?

Fund your DataBulge wallet by transferring to your personal virtual account number. Every top-up automatically reduces your outstanding balance.

Is DataBulge available everywhere?

DataBulge launched in Nigeria and is actively expanding.
