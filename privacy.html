5
How We Share Your Information
▼

We share your data only where strictly necessary:

  • Lenco (Bills API): We share your phone number and plan details with Lenco to deliver mobile data to your number. Lenco processes this as our service provider.
  • Paystack: We share your email and payment amount with Paystack to process wallet top-ups and Auto-Fund card charges. Paystack is PCI-DSS compliant and handles card data directly.
  • Supabase: Our database infrastructure provider. Your data is stored on Supabase servers in the EU (eu-west-1) under a data processing agreement.
  • Network Operators (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile): Your phone number is passed to the relevant operator solely to activate the data plan you purchased.
  • Law enforcement: Where required by a valid court order or Nigerian law.
We do NOT sell your data We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party — ever.
6
Data Storage & Security
▼

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase, eu-west-1 region). We implement the following measures to protect your data:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Your data is only accessible to your account — no other user can query it
  • Encrypted connections: All communication between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Password hashing: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods — we cannot see your password
  • Access controls: Only authorised Vortexedge personnel can access backend systems
  • Wallet protection: Wallet balances are protected by atomic database transactions to prevent unauthorised modification
  • Regular security reviews and patching

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses risk to your rights, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within the timeframe required by law.

7
Data Retention
▼

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as required by Nigerian law:

  • Account and profile data: Retained for the life of your account, plus 2 years after closure
  • Transaction records: Retained for 7 years as required by Nigerian financial regulations
  • Push notification tokens: Deleted when you uninstall the app or revoke permission
  • Support correspondence: Retained for 3 years

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time (see Section 9).
