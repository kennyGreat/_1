Terms & Conditions
⚠ Read carefully before creating your account.

1. WHAT DATABULGE IS

DataBulge is a technology platform by Vortexedge Limited that lets you buy mobile data bundles using a structured installment payment system. No data is delivered until full payment is completed.

2. WHAT DATABULGE IS NOT

DataBulge is NOT a financial institution, savings platform, investment vehicle, or thrift collector. Zero return on investment, interest, or profit-sharing of any kind.

3. HOW IT WORKS

Tap the +Fund Account button to fund your account. Once your account reaches the full plan cost, data is automatically delivered to your registered phone number.

4. SURCHARGE

3% for 7-day cycles. 5% for 28-day cycles. Non-refundable once subscription is created.

5. REFUND POLICY

If delivery fails, your wallet is automatically refunded within 4 minutes. Successfully delivered data is non-refundable.

6. ELIGIBILITY

  • Must be 18 or older
  • One account per person
  • Valid mobile number required

7. GOVERNING LAW

Governed by Nigerian law. DataBulge is operated by Vortexedge Limited, a Nigerian technology company serving users across Africa and the global diaspora. Contact: support@databulge.com
