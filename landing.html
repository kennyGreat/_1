Questions answered

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about DataBulge and how mobile data installments work.

What makes DataBulge different from other data apps?

DataBulge is the world's first platform that applies the installment payment model specifically to mobile data. Other apps require full upfront payment. DataBulge lets you activate your data immediately and pay the balance over time.

How do installment payments work?

You pay a small first installment to unlock your full data plan.Clear the remaining balance at your own pace. The data activates immediately you finish paying.

Which networks does DataBulge support?

In Nigeria, DataBulge currently supports MTN Nigeria, Airtel Nigeria, Glo Nigeria and 9mobile Nigeria. Expansion to more networks are actively underway.

Is there a credit check or income verification?

A phone number and an email is enough to get started.
